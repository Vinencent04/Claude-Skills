In 1932, Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight an army of birds. <break time="0.3s" />
And the birds won. <break time="0.9s" />
After the First World War, veterans became wheat farmers in Western Australia. <break time="0.6s" />
Then about twenty thousand emus came and started eating the crops. <break time="0.7s" />
The farmers asked for help. The government sent the army. <break time="0.7s" />
A major, two soldiers, two machine guns, and thousands of rounds. <break time="0.7s" />
But the emus split into small groups and scattered. <break time="0.6s" />
They put a machine gun on a truck. The truck was too slow, and the gun jammed. <break time="0.8s" />
After about a month, the army quietly went home. <break time="0.6s" />
The major said an army with the stamina of those birds could take on any army in the world. <break time="0.8s" />
The emus kept eating the wheat. <break time="1.0s" />
