# The War of the Bucket (1325), 54 s, voice only (ElevenLabs James), one continuous camera path (no cuts)
Documented: Battle of Zappolino, Nov 1325, Modena beat Bologna; a wooden bucket taken from a Bologna well is kept in Modena (Ghirlandina tower); Tassoni's mock-epic "La secchia rapita" (1622).
Flag / unverified: "two thousand men died" (commonly cited ~2,000 dead); "thirty thousand" Bolognese vs a fraction for Modena (sources differ strongly); the exact night-theft details and "Bologna still waiting" are narrative colour; whether Modena still shows the original or a copy.
