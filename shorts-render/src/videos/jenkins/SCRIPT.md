# The War of Jenkins' Ear (1731-1748), 93.7 s, voice only (ElevenLabs James, eleven_v3), accuracy-first: documented facts as facts, everything unsure shown as claim/legend on screen
Documented: 1731 the brig Rebecca (Capt. Robert Jenkins) was boarded by a Spanish guarda costa near Cuba (they hunted smugglers); Jenkins lost an ear (his account); 1738 Jenkins was questioned by Parliament; press/opposition demanded war; PM Robert Walpole was reluctant; Britain declared war on Spain in Oct 1739; the name "War of Jenkins' Ear" was coined by Thomas Carlyle in 1858; Vernon attacked Cartagena de Indias in 1741 with ~180 ships; Blas de Lezo had ~6 ships and ~3,000 men (one eye, one arm, one leg); Spain won, thousands of British died, mostly from disease; the war merged into the War of the Austrian Succession (to 1748); historians see the ear as a pretext, the real issue was trade.
