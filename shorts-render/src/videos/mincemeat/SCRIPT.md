# Operation Mincemeat (1943), 83.1 s, voice only (ElevenLabs James, eleven_v3), accuracy-first
Documented: British deception before the invasion of Sicily (Operation Husky, landings 10 July 1943); led by naval intelligence officer Ewen Montagu (with Charles Cholmondeley); the body of a homeless Welshman who died after eating rat poison was given the identity "Major William Martin, Royal Marines" with fiancée (photo/letters), theatre ticket stubs, a bank overdraft letter, and a briefcase chained to his wrist containing fake letters pointing at Greece and Sardinia; HMS Seraph released it off the Spanish coast on 30 April 1943; a Spanish fisherman found it; the papers reached German hands; the Germans believed them (Hitler ordered reinforcements to Greece and Sardinia, Rommel was sent to Greece); the Sicily landings caught them by surprise; "Martin" was buried in Spain; the man's real identity (Glyndwr Michael) was established in 1996; his headstone (1998 inscription) reads "Glyndwr Michael, served as Major William Martin, R.M."
Flag: "caught by surprise" is simplified (German forces in Sicily were still strong); the exact moment Rommel moved is simplified.
Staged colour (illustration only): all scenes and figures.
