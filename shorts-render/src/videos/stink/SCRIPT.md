## Facts (flag = verify)
- Summer 1858 was very hot; the Thames, which received London's raw sewage, stank; Parliament (Palace of Westminster) beside the river was affected. [Wikipedia "Great Stink"]
- Many Londoners drank Thames-derived water; miasma theory held that disease spread through foul smells. [Wikipedia]
- Curtains soaked in chloride of lime were hung at Parliament's windows. [Wikipedia]
- Parliament passed the bill funding the sewer rebuild in ~18 days (Metropolis Management Amendment Act, Aug 1858). [Wikipedia; flag exact count]
- Joseph Bazalgette's system: ~1,100 miles of street sewers (+82 miles of main), ~318 million bricks; cholera outbreaks stopped in London (last major in 1866 in the East End); many sewers are still in use. [Wikipedia; script says "over a thousand miles", "more than 300 million"]
- "Millions of lives were saved" is a loose summary. [flag]
