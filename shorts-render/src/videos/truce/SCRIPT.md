## Facts (flag = verify)
- Christmas Eve/Day 1914, Western Front: German soldiers lit small Christmas trees/candles on the trenches, sang carols (Stille Nacht); British soldiers answered. [Wikipedia "Christmas truce"]
- Next morning, men from both sides left their trenches, met in no man's land, shook hands, exchanged gifts (cigarettes, chocolate, buttons), buried dead together. [Wikipedia]
- Informal football kickabouts took place in places; organised full matches are doubted by historians: script says "hundreds of men started to play football" (flag: loose, "a ball" / kickabouts). [flag]
- Commanders were angry and ordered stronger measures against fraternisation; truces in 1915 were much smaller. [Wikipedia]
